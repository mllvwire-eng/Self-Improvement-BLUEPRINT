Before you start

Three levers, one system

Most people try to fix money, health, and mindset as three separate problems. They're not. Energy funds discipline, discipline funds income, and income removes the stress that was draining your energy in the first place. This blueprint moves through all three, in the order that actually compounds.

Pillar 01 — Mindset

Rebuild the operating system

You don't need more motivation. You need fewer decisions standing between you and the next right action.

1

Define one identity, not five goals

Pick who you're becoming this quarter. Every action gets judged against it.

2

Shrink the decision, not the ambition

Big goals stall on daily friction. Design the smallest version you can't skip.

3

Review weekly, not daily

Daily self-judgment burns you out. A weekly 10-minute review catches drift early.

Pillar 02 — Health

Protect the fuel supply

Nothing else on this list works if you're running on empty. Health isn't a side quest here — it's the budget everything else spends from.

1

Fix sleep before diet

A consistent wake time does more for energy and willpower than any meal plan.

2

Move daily, train weekly

A short daily walk or stretch keeps energy stable; save intensity for 3 sessions a week.

3

Build a floor, not a streak

Define the minimum you'll do on your worst day. Streaks break; floors hold.

Pillar 03 — Money

Turn capacity into income

With mindset and energy in place, income becomes a systems problem — not a hustle problem.

1

Audit before you optimize

Know exactly where every dollar goes before changing anything. Most leaks are invisible.

2

Build one income lever at a time

Pick a single skill, offer, or asset to develop for 90 days before adding a second.

3

Automate the boring 80%

Savings, bill pay, investing — remove willpower from anything that should be automatic.
